There are two parts. Sign **Part A** if you contribute as an individual. If you contribute on behalf of an employer or other legal entity, that entity signs **Part B** (and Part A is still needed from each individual who contributes, unless the entity's signature covers them as a Designated Employee).

## How to sign

- **Individuals (Part A):** the CLA Assistant bot comments on your first pull request. Reply with the exact sentence it asks for. This records your GitHub account, the date and this agreement's version, and applies to all your later contributions under this version.
- **Entities (Part B):** an authorized signatory emails a signed copy (including Schedule A, the list of Designated Employees by GitHub username) to **legal@docslurp.io**. We add the listed accounts to the allowlist and confirm by reply. Update Schedule A by email whenever people join or leave.
- If your employer may own rights in your work (employment contract, work for hire, a policy on side projects), get the employer to sign Part B or a written waiver **before** you contribute.

Questions: **legal@docslurp.io**.

## Definitions

"**Contribution**" means any original work of authorship, including any modification of or addition to an existing work, that you intentionally submit to DocSlurp for inclusion in, or documentation of, any DocSlurp project or repository. "Submit" means any form of electronic, verbal or written communication sent to DocSlurp or its representatives, including pull requests, patches, issues, mailing lists and code review discussion, but excluding communication that you conspicuously mark in writing as "Not a Contribution."

"**You**" means the individual who signs Part A, or in Part B the legal entity that signs and all entities that control, are controlled by or are under common control with it. "Control" means ownership of 50% or more of the voting interests or the power to direct management.

"**Project Licenses**" means the licenses under which DocSlurp distributes the part of the repository your Contribution modifies or joins, as in effect when you submit it, and any other license under which DocSlurp may distribute the project, including proprietary and commercial licenses.
