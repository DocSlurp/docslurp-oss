# Brightline Foods, Inc. — Minutes of the Board of Directors Meeting, September 18, 2026

## 1. Call to Order

A meeting of the Board of Directors of Brightline Foods, Inc. (the "Company") was held on September 18, 2026, in Portland, Oregon. Chair Elena Ortiz called the meeting to order at 9:02 a.m. and presided throughout. The Corporate Secretary recorded the minutes.

## 2. Attendance and Quorum

Seven of eight directors were present. The Corporate Secretary confirmed that a quorum was present and that the meeting was duly convened and could proceed with business.

| Name | Role | Attendance |
|---|---|---|
| Elena Ortiz | Chair | Present |
| Hannah Lee | Director | Present |
| Daniel Okafor | Chief Financial Officer (management attendee) | Present |
| Other directors | Directors | Six additional directors present; one director absent |

Members of senior management attended by invitation for relevant agenda items. Outside counsel attended the portion of the meeting concerning the acquisition.

## 3. Approval of Prior Minutes and Chair's Remarks

The directors reviewed the minutes of the prior regular meeting. No corrections were offered, and the minutes were approved as presented.

The Chair welcomed the directors and thanked them for their preparation. She noted that the agenda was substantial and reflected the Company's strong momentum, with three significant items for decision: a strategic acquisition, a capital investment in manufacturing capacity, and a capital return program for shareholders.

## 4. Chief Financial Officer's Report

Chief Financial Officer Daniel Okafor presented the Company's financial results for the third quarter. He reported Q3 revenue of $97.3 million, up 22% year over year. Mr. Okafor attributed the growth to strong demand across the Company's core product lines, improved retail distribution, and continued gains in the foodservice channel.

He also reviewed the Company's liquidity position and noted that the balance sheet remains healthy and well positioned to support the transactions on the day's agenda. Directors asked questions about margin trends, input costs, and working capital, and management responded in detail. The Chair thanked Mr. Okafor and his team for a clear and encouraging report.

## 5. Acquisition of Coastal Greens Co.

### 5.1 Presentation

Management presented the proposed acquisition of Coastal Greens Co. ("Coastal Greens"), a producer of fresh and packaged vegetable products with a strong regional brand and established customer relationships. Management described the strategic rationale, including complementary product lines, expanded geographic reach, and opportunities for procurement and logistics efficiencies.

Coastal Greens adds 3 production facilities and about 640 employees. Management emphasized the strength of the Coastal Greens team and the importance of a thoughtful integration plan that preserves its culture and operational expertise.

### 5.2 Discussion

Directors reviewed the valuation analysis, financing considerations, due diligence findings, and integration planning. Outside counsel summarized the regulatory review process and the principal terms of the definitive agreement. Directors asked about integration timing, retention of key personnel, and anticipated synergies, and were satisfied with management's responses.

### 5.3 Resolution

Upon motion duly made and seconded, the board **unanimously approved** the acquisition of Coastal Greens Co. for $214 million in cash. The acquisition is expected to close in Q4 2026, subject to regulatory approval. Management was authorized to finalize and execute the definitive agreement and related documents and to take all actions necessary to complete the transaction.

## 6. Sacramento Plant Expansion

Management presented a proposal to expand the Company's Sacramento plant to meet growing demand and improve production efficiency. The project includes new processing lines, additional warehousing, and upgraded energy-efficient equipment.

After discussion of project scope, timeline, and expected returns, the board approved a $35 million expansion of the Sacramento plant, adding 120 jobs. Directors noted the positive impact on the local community and the Company's commitment to workforce development. Management will provide periodic progress updates to the board.

## 7. Share Repurchase Program

Mr. Okafor presented a proposal for a share repurchase program, explaining that it reflects the board's confidence in the Company's long-term prospects and its balanced approach to capital allocation alongside growth investments.

Following discussion, the board authorized a share repurchase program of up to $50 million. Director Hannah Lee abstained from the vote; the motion passed 6–0 with one abstention. Repurchases may be made from time to time in the open market or through other permitted means, at management's discretion and subject to market conditions, applicable law, and the Company's liquidity needs.

## 9. Other Business

The Chair invited any further matters from the directors. The board briefly discussed the Company's sustainability initiatives and agreed to review a more detailed update at a future meeting. No other business was brought forward.

## 10. Next Meeting

The next meeting of the board is scheduled for December 11, 2026. Management will circulate board materials in advance, including an update on the Coastal Greens regulatory process and integration planning, as well as progress on the Sacramento expansion.
