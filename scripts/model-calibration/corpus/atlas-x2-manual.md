## 1. Safety Information

- Use the machine only with a grounded outlet supplying 220–240 V. The rated power of model AX2-220 is 1,800 W.
- Place the machine on a stable, level, heat-resistant surface with free space around the sides and top.
- Never immerse the machine, power cord, or plug in water or other liquids.
- Keep the steam wand and group head away from skin. Surfaces and steam are very hot during operation.
- Do not operate the machine with a damaged cord or plug. Contact customer support for service.
- Unplug the machine before cleaning or when it will not be used for an extended period.
- This appliance is intended for household and light office use. Children should be supervised near it.

## 3. Machine Overview

The Atlas X2 uses a true dual-boiler design, which lets you brew and steam at the same time without waiting for temperatures to recover.

- **Brew boiler:** 0.75 L, dedicated to espresso extraction for stable temperature.
- **Steam boiler:** 1.5 L, providing dry, powerful steam for milk texturing.
- **Water tank:** 2.4 liters, removable, with a carry handle and a lid.
- **Group head:** commercial-style, with pre-infusion for even extraction.
- **Display and controls:** a clear screen with an OK button, navigation dial, and dedicated buttons for single shot, double shot, steam, and hot water.
- **Drip tray:** removable, with a float indicator that rises when the tray is nearly full.

## 4. First-Time Setup

1. **Unpack and inspect.** Remove all packaging and check for visible damage.
2. **Rinse components.** Wash the tank, portafilter, baskets, and pitcher with warm water and mild soap. Rinse well.
3. **Install the water filter cartridge.** Soak the cartridge as directed on its packaging, then insert it into the tank's filter holder.
4. **Fill the tank.** Fill with fresh, cold water up to the MAX line, then seat the tank firmly.
5. **Connect power.** Plug into a grounded 220–240 V outlet and switch the machine on.
6. **Prime the system.** Place a container under the group head and press the hot water button until water flows steadily. Then open the steam wand briefly until water and steam emerge.
7. **Warm up.** Allow the machine to heat fully. The display will indicate when it is ready.

Run a few blank shots with the empty portafilter locked in place to pre-heat the group and cups before your first drink.

---

## 5. Making Espresso

1. Select a basket (single or double) and insert it into the portafilter.
2. Grind fresh beans to a fine, consistent texture, and dose evenly.
3. Tamp level with firm, even pressure.
4. Lock the portafilter into the group head.
5. Place a pre-warmed cup beneath the spouts.
6. Press the single or double shot button.

The Atlas X2 is tuned for a recommended brew pressure of 9 bar at the group head. If your shot runs too fast, grind finer; if it runs slowly or chokes, grind slightly coarser or reduce the dose.

### Adjusting Brew Temperature

The default brew temperature is 93 °C, a balanced starting point for most medium roasts. To change it:

1. Open the settings menu using the navigation dial.
2. Select **Brew Temp**.
3. Turn the dial to choose a value from 88 to 96 °C in 0.5 °C steps.
4. Press OK to confirm.

Lighter roasts often taste best at the higher end of the range, while darker roasts can be sweeter and less bitter at the lower end.

---

## 6. Steaming Milk

1. Fill the pitcher about one third with cold milk.
2. Press the steam button and wait for the ready indicator.
3. Purge the wand briefly into the drip tray.
4. Submerge the tip just below the milk surface to introduce air, then lower the pitcher slightly to create a gentle whirlpool.
5. Stop when the pitcher feels hot to the touch.
6. Turn off steam, wipe the wand with a damp cloth, and purge again.

Because the steam boiler is separate, you can pull a shot and steam milk simultaneously.

---

## 7. Hot Water

Place a cup beneath the wand and press the hot water button. Press again to stop. This is ideal for tea or for preparing an Americano.

---

## 8. Eco Mode

Eco mode helps conserve energy. When it is enabled, the machine enters standby after 45 minutes of inactivity. Press any button to wake it; allow a short time for the boilers to return to temperature. You can enable or disable Eco mode in the settings menu.

## 9. Care and Maintenance

Regular maintenance keeps your machine performing well and extends its life.

### Maintenance Schedule

| Task | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Backflush the group with detergent | Weekly |
| Replace water filter cartridge | Every 60 days |
| Descale (soft water) | Every 3 months |
| Descale (hard water, above 8 °dH) | Monthly |
| Empty and rinse drip tray | Daily |
| Wipe steam wand | After every use |

### Backflushing

1. Insert the blind basket into the portafilter and add a small amount of espresso machine detergent.
2. Lock the portafilter into the group head.
3. Start a brew cycle for about 10 seconds, then stop. Repeat several times.
4. Remove the portafilter, rinse it, and repeat the cycle with the blind basket and plain water to flush residue.

Perform this procedure with detergent weekly.

### Descaling

Scale buildup reduces heating performance and flavor. Descale every 3 months if you use soft water, or monthly if your water is hard (above 8 °dH).

1. Empty the tank and remove the water filter cartridge.
2. Mix descaling solution according to its instructions and fill the tank.
3. Place a large container under the group head and steam wand.
4. Start the descale program from the settings menu and follow the on-screen prompts.
5. When finished, rinse the tank thoroughly, refill with fresh water, and run water through the group head and wand until clear.
6. Reinstall the filter cartridge.

### Replacing the Water Filter

Replace the water filter cartridge every 60 days. Remove the old cartridge, soak the new one as directed, and fit it into the holder. Reset the reminder in the settings menu if available.

### Daily Cleaning

Rinse the tank, empty the drip tray, and wipe the exterior with a soft, damp cloth. Do not use abrasive cleaners. Remove the portafilter basket after use and rinse out coffee grounds.

## 10. Display Codes and Troubleshooting

| Code | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| E-07 | Water tank is empty | Refill the tank and press OK |
| E-12 | Descaling is due | Run the descaling procedure in Section 9 |

### Common Issues

| Problem | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Weak, watery espresso | Grind too coarse or dose too low | Grind finer or increase dose |
| Slow or no flow | Grind too fine, clogged basket | Grind coarser, clean basket |
| Little steam | Wand tip blocked | Clear tip with a pin; descale if needed |
| Machine will not heat | Standby active or power issue | Press any button; check outlet |
| Water on counter | Drip tray overfull or tank misseated | Empty tray; reseat tank |

If a problem persists, switch the machine off, unplug it, and contact customer support.

If you will not use the machine for a long time, empty the tank, drip tray, and boilers as far as possible, clean all parts, and store the unit in a dry place. At end of life, dispose of the machine at an appropriate electrical-recycling facility rather than with household waste.
