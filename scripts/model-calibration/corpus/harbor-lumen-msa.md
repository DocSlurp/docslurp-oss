# Master Services Agreement between Harbor & Pine Logistics LLC and Lumen Robotics, Inc.

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of February 1, 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between **Harbor & Pine Logistics LLC** ("Harbor & Pine") and **Lumen Robotics, Inc.** ("Lumen"). Harbor & Pine and Lumen are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

## Background

Harbor & Pine operates distribution and fulfillment facilities and wishes to modernize its material handling operations. Lumen designs and operates warehouse automation robots and the software that coordinates them. The Parties wish to establish a long-term, collaborative relationship on the terms below.

## 1. Definitions

- **"Services"** means the warehouse automation robots and fleet software that Lumen provides to Harbor & Pine under this Agreement, together with related installation, support, and maintenance.
- **"Fleet"** means all robots deployed at Harbor & Pine sites under this Agreement.
- **"Site"** means a Harbor & Pine facility at which Services are deployed.
- **"Order Form"** means a written document signed by both Parties describing the Sites, robot counts, and any special terms.
- **"Monthly Fee"** means the monthly subscription fee described in Section 4.

## 2. Services

2.1 **Provision of Services.** Lumen will provide the Services to Harbor & Pine in accordance with this Agreement and each Order Form.

2.2 **Standard of Performance.** Lumen will perform the Services with professional skill and care, using qualified personnel, and in compliance with applicable law and Harbor & Pine's reasonable site safety rules.

2.3 **Software Updates.** Lumen will provide updates and enhancements to the fleet software at no additional charge and will schedule disruptive updates, where practicable, outside peak operating hours in coordination with Harbor & Pine.

2.4 **Harbor & Pine Responsibilities.** Harbor & Pine will provide reasonable site access, power, network connectivity, and floor conditions suitable for robot operation, and will designate a primary operations contact.

## 3. Term and Renewal

3.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 36 months (the "Initial Term").

3.2 **Automatic Renewal.** After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive 12-month terms (each a "Renewal Term"; together with the Initial Term, the "Term").

3.3 **Non-Renewal.** Either Party may prevent renewal by giving written notice to the other at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term.

3.4 **Transition Assistance.** Upon expiration or termination, Lumen will reasonably cooperate in an orderly wind-down, including the safe removal of robots and the export of Harbor & Pine's data in a commonly used format.

## 4. Fees and Payment

4.1 **Monthly Subscription Fee.** Harbor & Pine will pay Lumen a monthly subscription fee of $128,500 for the Services described in the initial Order Form.

4.2 **Invoicing and Payment.** Lumen will invoice the Monthly Fee monthly in advance. Undisputed invoices are payable net 30 from the invoice date.

4.3 **Annual Adjustment.** Lumen may increase fees once per year, and any annual fee increase is capped at 4%. Lumen will give Harbor & Pine written notice of any increase reasonably in advance of its effective date.

4.4 **Disputed Amounts.** Harbor & Pine will notify Lumen promptly of any good-faith dispute, pay the undisputed portion, and the Parties will work together to resolve the dispute quickly.

4.5 **Taxes.** Fees exclude applicable sales, use, and similar taxes, which Harbor & Pine will pay, excluding taxes based on Lumen's net income.

## 5. Service Levels

5.1 **Uptime Commitment.** Lumen commits to 99.7% monthly fleet availability, measured across the Fleet each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance agreed with Harbor & Pine and downtime caused by Harbor & Pine's acts or omissions, site power or network failures, or force majeure events.

5.2 **Service Credits.** If monthly fleet availability falls below the commitment, Harbor & Pine will receive a service credit of 5% of the Monthly Fee for each 0.5 percentage point below the commitment, capped at 25% of the Monthly Fee.

5.3 **Application of Credits.** Credits will be applied against the next invoice, or paid within a reasonable time if no further invoice is due. Credits are Harbor & Pine's primary remedy for availability shortfalls, without limiting its termination rights under Section 9.

5.4 **Reporting.** Lumen will deliver a monthly availability report within a reasonable period after month end, and the Parties will review performance in periodic business reviews.

### Service Credit Illustration

| Availability shortfall below commitment | Credit (% of Monthly Fee) |
|---|---|
| Up to 0.5 percentage point | 5% |
| Up to 1.0 percentage point | 10% |
| Up to 1.5 percentage points | 15% |
| Up to 2.0 percentage points | 20% |
| 2.5 percentage points or more | 25% (maximum) |

## 6. Security and Data

6.1 **Ownership.** Harbor & Pine owns all data generated from or about its operations, including inventory, order, and facility data ("Customer Data"). Lumen owns the robots, fleet software, and related intellectual property.

6.2 **Use of Data.** Lumen will use Customer Data only to provide and improve the Services and may use de-identified, aggregated operational data for product development.

6.3 **Safeguards.** Lumen will maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards consistent with industry standards and will notify Harbor & Pine without undue delay after becoming aware of a security incident affecting Customer Data.

## 7. Confidentiality

Each Party will protect the other's non-public information with at least reasonable care, use it only to perform or receive the Services, and disclose it only to personnel and advisors who need to know and are bound by comparable obligations. These duties do not apply to information that is public through no fault of the recipient, independently developed, or lawfully received from a third party, and they survive for a reasonable period after the Term.

## 8. Warranties, Indemnity, and Insurance

8.1 **Mutual Authority.** Each Party represents that it is duly organized and authorized to enter into this Agreement.

8.2 **Lumen Warranties.** Lumen warrants that the Services will conform in all material respects to their documentation and that the robots will be maintained in safe working order.

8.3 **Indemnity.** Lumen will defend and indemnify Harbor & Pine against third-party claims alleging that the Services infringe intellectual property rights. Each Party will defend and indemnify the other against third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage caused by its negligence or willful misconduct.

8.4 **Insurance.** Each Party will maintain commercial general liability, workers' compensation, and cyber liability insurance at commercially reasonable levels and provide certificates on request.

## 9. Termination

9.1 **For Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or an Order Form if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure within a reasonable cure period after written notice.

9.2 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate on written notice if the other becomes insolvent or subject to bankruptcy proceedings that are not promptly dismissed.

9.3 **Effect.** Termination does not affect accrued payment obligations or provisions intended to survive.

## 10. Limitation of Liability

10.1 **Liability Cap.** Each Party's total liability arising out of this Agreement will not exceed the total fees paid in the 12 months before the claim.

10.2 **Excluded Damages.** Neither Party will be liable for indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages, or lost profits, arising out of this Agreement.

10.3 **Exceptions.** The limits in this Section 10 do not apply to breaches of confidentiality, indemnification obligations, gross negligence, willful misconduct, or liability that cannot be limited by law.

## 11. Expansion

11.1 **Additional Capacity.** Harbor & Pine may request additional robots at existing Sites through a new Order Form, and Lumen will use commercially reasonable efforts to deliver them promptly.

11.2 **Additional Sites.** Harbor & Pine may expand the Services to additional Sites at the same per-robot rate of $2,150 per robot per month. Each expansion will be documented in an Order Form that specifies the Site, robot count, and deployment schedule, and will be governed by this Agreement.

11.3 **Co-Termination.** Unless an Order Form states otherwise, expansion Order Forms will run concurrently with the Term.

## 12. Governing Law and Disputes

12.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

12.2 **Escalation.** The Parties will first attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith discussions between senior executives before pursuing formal remedies.

12.3 **Venue.** Subject to Section 12.2, the state and federal courts located in Delaware will have exclusive jurisdiction over disputes arising from this Agreement, and each Party consents to that jurisdiction.

## 14. General Provisions

14.1 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, with its Order Forms, is the entire agreement of the Parties on its subject and supersedes prior discussions and understandings.

14.2 **Order of Precedence.** If an Order Form conflicts with this Agreement, the Order Form controls only if it expressly identifies the provision it modifies.

14.3 **Amendment and Waiver.** Amendments must be in writing and signed by both Parties. A failure to enforce any provision is not a waiver.

14.4 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, except to a successor in a merger or sale of substantially all of its relevant business.

14.5 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delays caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it uses reasonable efforts to resume performance.

14.6 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors, and nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, or agency.

14.7 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original.
