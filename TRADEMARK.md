2. **Compatibility statements.** "Works with DocSlurp", "DocSlurp-compatible", "integrates with DocSlurp", "DocSlurp plugin for X", as long as the product's own name leads and the statement is true.
3. **Unmodified redistribution.** Redistributing the unmodified software, in source or binary form, with its LICENSE, NOTICE and this trademark notice intact, under the name it ships with. Running the unmodified software for your own use under its own name.
